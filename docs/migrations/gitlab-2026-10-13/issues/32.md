## Description

`do_stat()` (`src/act/act.wizard.c`, the `stat file` branch) builds a body for a player who is not
online with `CREATE`, `clear_char()`, `player_specials` and `load_char()`, puts it in room rnum 0
with `char_to_room_cause()`, shows it, and then calls `extract_char(victim)`.

`extract_char()` only flags the character and counts a pending extraction. `extract_pending_chars()`
walks `character_list` for the flagged ones, and the body was never put on `character_list`
(only `create_char()`, `read_mobile()` and the login path add to it). So at the end of the pulse the
count is still one, `extract_pending_chars()` logs
`SYSERR: extract_pending_chars() - 1 extraction(s) still pending after processing character_list.`
and resets it, and the body stays where it was put: in the people list of room 0, with no
descriptor, until the next reboot. Its `char_data`, `player_specials` and action queues are never
freed.

Found while answering the review of MR !27 (finding 2 of the `object-save-fixes-p4` review, which
assumed the body reached `extract_char_final()`; with `get_char_bag()` answering NULL for a body
without bag storage, it would survive that too). The other `load_char()` callers that build a body
(`do_last`, `do_set`, the clan commands) free it with `free_char()` without a room.

Fix: take the body out of the room and free it in place, `char_from_room(victim); free_char(victim);`,
in place of the `extract_char(victim)` call, and a test that runs `stat file` for a player who is
not online and checks that room 0 holds nobody afterwards.
