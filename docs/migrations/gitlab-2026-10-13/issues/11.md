## Description

## Problem

Vessel waypoints (`ship_waypoints`) and routes (`ship_routes`, `ship_route_waypoints`) are one
pool shared by every player, and the schema does not record who created a row. Any captain at a
helm, or the owner anywhere aboard, can `delwaypoint` or `delroute` any of them.

S10 (branch `feat/vessels-s10`, commit `523417bbd`) closed the harmful case only: `delwaypoint`
refuses a waypoint that any route sails through, and `delroute` refuses a route that a hull runs on
a schedule or is sailing (under way, waiting or paused). The public ferries and merchants run on
schedules, so their routes and waypoints are safe.

Still open: a route a player has saved but is not sailing, and a waypoint on no route, can be
deleted by anyone. A rival can wipe another captain's saved routes between voyages.

## Proposed fix

- Add a creator column to `ship_waypoints` and `ship_routes` (player name or ID, empty for staff
  and content rows), in a new vessel schema phase with rollback and verifier SQL, `master_schema.sql`
  and the boot ensure functions.
- `setwaypoint` and `createroute` record the creator; `delwaypoint` and `delroute` allow the
  creator and immortals only, and keep the S10 in-use refusals.
- Existing rows: leave the creator empty (staff-only deletion) or backfill where it can be
  inferred; decide in the plan.
- Production-linked CuTest coverage, DELWAYPOINT and DELROUTE help in both places,
  `VESSEL_SYSTEM.md`.

## Why not in S10

S10 is the player guide; the guard it added needs no schema change. Ownership needs a migration
and a decision on legacy rows.
