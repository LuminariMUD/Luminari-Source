## Description

Follow-up from !1, which replaced the four-arm flag with an arm count. The open items are recorded in `docs/ongoing-projects/EXTRA_LIMB_MECHANICS.md#open-balance-work`.

- Extra Arms still costs 3 race points per rank, the price set when a rank was one extra swing. A rank is now one full arm. The doc suggests 7.5 RP for each rank that adds the third or fourth arm and 1 RP for each rank past four, but equal pricing is not established: the third arm alone opens lower gloves and sleeves.
- Pricing for mixed Four Arms, Extra Arms and race adjustments is undecided, and so is whether the Thri-Kreen cap exception applies.
- Four Arms stays at 15 RP. The unarmed monk third hand raises its value for monks, and that value is not priced.
- Before deploying: Extra Arms grants on saved characters or staff-built items now mean arms instead of swings. Check them.

**Decisions**, written into `docs/guides/PLAYER_RACES_REFERENCE.md`:

- An arm is priced by what it opens, not by the rank: the third arm 9 RP (lower sleeves, lower
  gloves, a third wrist, and a weapon hand that mirrors the first pair's whole primary chain),
  the fourth 6 RP (a fourth wrist and the hand that makes the pair dual, its trained swings
  behind feats), each arm past four 1 RP. The two add up to Four Arms' 15 RP: the worn positions
  split three to one and the weapon hands' swings five candidates to four, so 11 RP of weapon
  hands split 6 to 5.
- Mixed sources: a race is priced by the arm count it ends with, whatever mix of Four Arms,
  Extra Arms and `arm_adjust` produces it. A negative `arm_adjust` is a drawback by the
  lost-slot row.
- The single-trait cap exemption stays Thri-Kreen's Four Arms alone; another race taking arms
  (over the cap in every tier but Epic quest) records its own exemption in composition rule 2.
- The monk's empty third hand is the swing a weapon there makes (`plan_second_pair()` plans one
  third-hand chain, set by either, and no candidate of its own): class interaction, not priced,
  inside the 15 RP. Thri-Kreen stays at 20.0 RP.

Corrected 2026-10-08 after the review of MR !29: the house audit now reads `house_data`, the
table the game loads a house from (the `.house` file is only its existence check), and the
world-file recipe in the plan takes two-field and CR-terminated `A` records. The result is
unchanged.
