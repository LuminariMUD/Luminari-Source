## Description

## Problem

`scripts/vessels/provision_vessel_harbor.sh` stops at its NPC merchant check whenever the
Harbor Sandbox Merchant's schedule is disabled, and nothing in the script or the game enables
it again.

- The server disables a schedule by itself: a scheduled departure whose route fails the
  traversal check clears `SCHEDULE_FLAG_ENABLED` and saves it
  (`schedule_trigger_departure()` in `src/vessels/vessels_autopilot.c`, log line "Disabled
  untraversable scheduled route"). A hull stalled off her route gets there too.
- The harbor provisioner's merchant check requires `schedule.enabled = 1` and only reads it.
  `scripts/vessels/provision_vessel_campaign.sh` has the same situation for the Vailand
  merchant and repairs it: it re-applies the route and sets `ship_schedules.enabled = 1`.
- In the development database the harbor merchant's schedule has been disabled since
  2026-09-29, and she is battered (armor 0, rudder 1 of 20). Every gate batch since then
  enabled the schedule by hand in its disposable copy of the database before running the
  provisioner or the merchant gate.

## Completion criteria

- `provision_vessel_harbor.sh` restores the harbor merchant to service on a database where her
  schedule was disabled (as the campaign provisioner does for the Vailand merchant), or fails
  with a message that names the cause and the fix.
- The Shared Harbor merchant gate (`scripts/vessels/test_vessel_merchant_in_game.sh`) passes on
  a fresh copy of the development database with no manual SQL first.
- `docs/testing/VESSEL_SYSTEM_TESTING.md` ("Shared Harbor Fast Check") says what the
  provisioner does about a disabled schedule.
