## Description

## Problem

The vessel release requirements ask for the complete vessel subsystem to stay within 25 ms per
game tick at 500 vessels (`docs/product-requirements/VESSEL_SYSTEM_REQUIREMENTS.md`, section 6
"Performance" and release acceptance item 5). That gate has no current measurement and no
current threshold:

- `scripts/vessels/run_vessel_scale_benchmark.sh start` refuses to run: "The fleet-heartbeat
  benchmark is retired. Measure native vessel owner events before defining new scale
  thresholds."
- `docs/testing/VESSEL_BENCHMARKS.md` ("Current Verdict") says the native 500-hull measurement
  taken on the S2 momentum model is the new scale baseline, and that "a native per-tick
  threshold replacing the retired runner's 25 ms p95 gate is not yet defined".
- That measurement (section "S2 Momentum Re-baseline": 500 hulls, mean 1.34 ms per 500-hull
  tick) was taken on 2026-09-29, before the damage model, gunnery, crew stamina, crew repairs,
  and raider AI were added to the per-tick work. Nothing was measured afterwards, and the ferry
  soak was not rerun either.
- `docs/systems/VESSEL_SYSTEM.md` ("Performance Characteristics") says the final preflight must
  repeat the full scale gate on the release candidate.
- The gate table's "Development mechanics and final preflight" row is marked Complete on the
  strength of the older 500-vessel run.

## Completion criteria

- A native per-tick threshold is defined from the vessel owner-event profiler (the measurement
  procedure is in `VESSEL_BENCHMARKS.md`, "S2 Momentum Re-baseline").
- The 500-hull measurement is repeated on current master and recorded in
  `VESSEL_BENCHMARKS.md` against that threshold.
- `run_vessel_scale_benchmark.sh` either runs the native measurement or is removed, with its
  parser test.
- The gate table row and the `VESSEL_SYSTEM.md` performance section state the current evidence.
