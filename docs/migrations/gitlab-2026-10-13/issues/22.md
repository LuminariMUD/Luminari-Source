## Description

## Problem

Release acceptance item 6 in `docs/product-requirements/VESSEL_SYSTEM_REQUIREMENTS.md` asks
that "every documented operational failure mode has a tested staff or command-line recovery
procedure". Four vessel cases hand the problem to the staff with no command and no written
procedure:

1. **A plunder bounty that could not be recorded.** `plunder` logs to the staff: "...'s bounty
   of N gold for plundering ship ... could not be recorded; it has to be applied by hand."
   (`src/vessels/vessels_piracy.c`, `do_plunder`). No staff command sets or adds a bounty:
   `bounty` has only `pay`, so "by hand" means SQL on `vessel_bounties` against a running
   server.
2. **A trade-in that failed and could not be undone.** `shipbuy <id> trade` tells the captain
   "Your N gold stays paid until the staff settle it" and logs "the database may hold the new
   hull: the N gold charged is kept and has to be settled by hand." (`src/vessels/vessels_edit.c`).
   Nothing says how to tell which hull the rows hold or how to return the gold.
3. **A freight delivery whose outcome is unknown.** When neither the delivery nor its undo can
   be confirmed, `contractdeliver` logs "Freight contract N of <player> may be recorded as
   delivered without its N gold payout" (`src/vessels/vessels_contracts.c`). There is no
   procedure for checking `freight_contracts` and paying or reopening the contract.
4. **Another player's waypoint or route of the same name.** Waypoints and routes are looked up
   by name: the caller's own row, then a harbor row (creator 0), then the newest
   (`src/vessels/vessels_autopilot.c`). An immortal who must delete or inspect one player's
   `x` cannot choose it when the immortal or the harbor also has an `x`: `delroute` and
   `delwaypoint` take only a name.

`docs/systems/VESSEL_SYSTEM.md` describes each message but no recovery, and the
troubleshooting section has no entry for them.

## Completion criteria

- A staff command, or a documented and tested command-line procedure, for each of the four
  cases: apply a bounty, settle a trade-in the rows may hold, settle a delivery of unknown
  outcome, and address a waypoint or route by ID or by creator.
- The procedures are in `VESSEL_SYSTEM.md` ("Troubleshooting" or "Operations") and, for
  commands, in help (`lib/text/help/help.hlp` and the database).
- Each is exercised once: a test for a new command, a recorded run for a written procedure.
