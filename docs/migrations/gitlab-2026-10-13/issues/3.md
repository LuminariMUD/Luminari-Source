## Description

Follow-up from !2 (merged as 2d7923cac, work item #2). The five Duris descend forms are registered, hard-locked, and convertible through a standard quest race reward. No quest awards one yet, so nobody can become a form.

## What to build

Each form needs a quest line whose final quest sets the race reward to the form's ID. Model it on the Vampire line in zone 347 (`lib/world/qst/347.qst`): quests 34700-34721 from questmaster mobile 29906 (Erich) are chained through previous/next, and the final quest, 34721, carries race reward 46.

`descend_form_conversions[]` in `src/quest/quest.c` owns the conversion. When the final quest completes, it requires:

- level 30;
- levels in one of the form's classes;
- no group, leader, or followers;
- a real race that is not already a Lich, Vampire, or descend form.

It then respecs the character to the form's class, sets experience to 0 and alignment to -1000, and saves.

| Form | ID | Class levels required | Respec to | Duris lore |
| -- | -- | -- | -- | -- |
| Death Knight | 55 | Blackguard or Warrior | Blackguard | Fallen paladins raised to undeath; the anti-paladin form |
| Wight | 56 | Warrior | Warrior | Duris's old descend made level 35+ necromancers into Wights (as Warriors) |
| Revenant | 57 | Warrior or Rogue | Warrior | Corpses driven by revenge; Duris's old descend (as Mercenaries) |
| Shadow Beast | 58 | Rogue or Assassin | Rogue | Duris let level 50 Revenants become Shadow Beasts (Assassin) |
| Phantom | 59 | Wizard, Summoner, or Psionicist | Wizard | Duris let level 50 Vampires become Phantoms (Conjurer); here a Vampire is refused |

QEDIT and `wtool` already accept race rewards 55-59 (`docs/world_game-data/QUEST_FILE_FORMAT.md`). Each form's stats, feats, and lore are in the "Descend forms" rows of `docs/guides/PLAYER_RACES_REFERENCE.md` and its `RACE-<FORM>` help topic.
