## Description

## Problem

The engineering work on the vessel system is finished: the DurisMUD naval model (steps S1-S15,
merge requests !6 through !22) is merged, and `docs/adr/0003-durismud-naval-model.md` records its
decisions. Three release gates remain in the "Release Gate State" table of
`docs/product-requirements/VESSEL_SYSTEM_REQUIREMENTS.md`, and no work item tracked them:

| Gate | State | Exit condition in the table |
| -- | -- | -- |
| Player-data balance | Open | Collect real beta data, then tune combat time-to-kill, crew hire prices, freight margins, refit costs, insurance, and dock fees |
| Structured human beta | Open | Validate first-hour discovery, multiplayer roles, builder independence, a supervised NPC-shipping sample within one hour, and at least 70% "fun" combat feedback |
| Staged production rollout | Blocked | After balance and beta sign-off: staff, then a beta cohort, then all players, with rollback authority, monitoring, the announcement, and a postmortem |

## Current evidence

- Every balance number is a DurisMUD value translated into LuminariMUD's units (2 gold per
  platinum, speed times 0.3, hull level times 30/56). None has been measured on LuminariMUD
  players. ADR 0003 lists the anchors the numbers were derived from ("Balance anchors").
- `vesseldebug balance` (`src/vessels/vessels_balance.c`) checks the equal-warship duel against
  decision D2 (3-8 minute median, 12 minute p95, nothing under 90 seconds) and the 1,000-trade
  simulation. It is mechanical evidence only, and its last line reads "Human beta and player fun
  sign-off: REQUIRED".
- The one full play-through so far (step S9, 2026-10-02) was a single operator playing two mortal
  captains through the web client, with a staff character staging each scenario. Two paths could
  not be played and have unit-test coverage only:
  - renown and prize money shared among allied hulls in sight (needs a third captain);
  - an ordinary creature encounter at sea (the development sandbox's only encounter row is the
    Admiralty hunter patrol).
- The vessel MSDP variables were exercised in the Luminari Web client and over a raw MSDP
  connection (`scripts/vessels/test_vessel_client_in_game.sh`). Mudlet and LuminariGUI were left
  out of the play test.
- Hull wear and weather damage kept their absolute points when the damage model enlarged armor
  and sails (`vessel_upkeep_tick()` in `src/vessels/vessels_upgrades.c`, storm damage in
  `src/vessels/vessels_hazards.c`), so they now matter proportionally less. They were left as
  an input to this gate, not tuned.
- The gate table says general production release is not approved while a row is Open or Blocked.
  The deploy record of 2026-10-04 has production running the current vessel code, schema, and
  content (harbor sandbox, public ferry, Vailand merchant, Blackwake derelict, raider and
  contraband content). The table does not say what is live or under what limits, so the rollout
  row and the deployed state need reconciling.
- `docs/development/CONSIDERATIONS.md` ("Vessel System", Active Concerns) and the "Release
  Status" header of `docs/systems/VESSEL_SYSTEM.md` repeat the open gates.

## Completion criteria

- A beta with real players is run and its results recorded: first-hour discovery, multiplayer
  crew roles (including a fight with at least three captains, so the allied renown split is
  played), builder independence, a supervised NPC-shipping sample inside one hour, a creature
  encounter at sea, and a combat "fun" rating from the respondents.
- Combat time to kill, crew hire prices, freight margins, refit costs, insurance, and dock fees
  are tuned from that data, or confirmed unchanged, with the evidence recorded in
  `docs/testing/VESSEL_BENCHMARKS.md`. Weapon reload time is the agreed tuning lever for time to
  kill (ADR 0003).
- The rollout row states what production runs and the stages still to come, or is closed after
  the staged rollout.
- Each of the three rows in the gate table is Complete with its evidence, or re-scoped by the
  product owner, and `CONSIDERATIONS.md` and the `VESSEL_SYSTEM.md` header say the same.
