## Description

Found in the live check of #4 (its item 29); still true on master `962463fa6`.

The artificer feat `FEAT_WEIRD_SCIENCE` (`src/character/feats.c:5862-5867`) says its devices are
temporary with a limited number of uses per day. In the code, devices are permanent (the saved
`duration` field is never read) and regain one use per tick out of combat (`check_device_one()`,
`src/core/limits.c`). The device help entry already states the real rules.
