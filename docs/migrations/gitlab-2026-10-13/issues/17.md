## Description

**Defect.** A sheath's weapons hang off the sheath object (`sheath_primary`, `sheath_secondary`) and are handled only by the player save and by `sheath` / `unsheath`. Two paths lose track of them.

1. **Pet saves.** `objsave_save_obj_record_db_pet()` writes no sheath rows. A loaded sheath given to a pet comes back empty when the pet is restored, and its weapons are in no row.
2. **Extraction.** `extract_obj()` does not look at a sheath's weapons. Extracting a loaded sheath (junk, a shop sale, the extraction after a rent save) frees the sheath and leaves the weapons in `object_list` with no holder. After a rent save that is a memory leak only, since the rows were written. After a junk or a sale the weapons are gone from play without having been destroyed.

**Not part of this.** A loaded sheath lying in a house: MR !20 (review P1) made the house save store its weapons as the house's own objects.

**Fix.** Give the pet writer the sheath rows the player writer has (or refuse a loaded sheath on a pet), and make `extract_obj()` extract what a sheath holds.

Found while fixing the review of MR !20 (S13); read from the code, not played. Not changed there.

Closed by MR !28, merged on 2026-10-08 as merge commit `05c76111f`. The plan's record is closed in
`55c934d5c`.

- `59bc44f45`: `extract_obj()` extracts what a sheath holds, beside the loop that extracts a
  container's contents. Junking or selling a loaded sheath destroys its weapons with it, and the
  leak after a rent save ends.
- `2b8959cdd`: `Crash_save_pet()` writes a sheath's weapons as the pet's own objects beside the
  sheath, as a house save does: no new table and no migration. A worn sheath's weapons are written
  as inventory records, a carried sheath's at its own depth. The restored pet carries the weapons
  and an empty sheath.
- Review, `079e9bd6e`: a sheathed object knows its sheath (`sheathed_in`). `obj_to_sheath()` and
  `obj_from_sheath()` set and clear the slot and the back-pointer together, so an object extracted
  on its own (a deleted prototype, a timer that runs out) leaves its slot empty instead of a freed
  address. `objs_from_sheath()`, which had no caller, is removed.
- Review, `8a8f0fdd7`: `artifact_on_extract()` counts a sheathed object as placed, so a sheathed
  artifact's destruction releases its ownership and is counted in the ledger.
- Review, `da68e251a`: `delete_object()` hands a deleted sheath's weapons to its holder beside the
  contents.
- `docs/systems/SAVE_SYSTEMS_BREAKDOWN.md` has the pet rule, in the pets section.
- Not changed here: the player sheath loader's debug trace (#33), `otransform`'s copy over a live
  object (#34), and `Crash_extract_norents()`, which still does not walk a sheath's weapons.
- No deploy: the fix reaches production with the next release.
