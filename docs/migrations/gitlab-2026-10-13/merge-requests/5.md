## Description

Fixes the reported `device add` bug and the other 25 artificer defects listed in the work item.

## Reported bug

`device add` compared a class spell level against the circle cap, so a level 20 artificer could not add any 3rd- or 4th-circle spell. It now converts to a circle like `device create`, charges the same per-circle slot budget, and stores the assigned level.

## Other items (work item numbering)

- **1** `find_skill_num()` and `find_ability_num()` bound each copied word (stack overflow from a long spell word).
- **2** Device saves keep the DC penalty and broken flag on the existing line; old files load them as 0.
- **3** `device use` stops once the target dies or leaves the room.
- **4** Metamagic on wand/staff objects spends 1 + the metamagic cost in charges.
- **5** Improved Metamagic Science UMD check can fail; the DC comes from the spell circle.
- **6** `device create cancel` and `device repair cancel` pass the busy check.
- **7** Skills are not device spells.
- **8** Elbow Grease and Jack of All Trades reach craft, refine, harvest, brew, and golem rolls.
- **9** Artificers get the alchemist starting kit.
- **10** Construct Stone Golem at 15, Construct Iron Golem at 20.
- **11** Brilliance and Blunder is a gnome racial next to Gnomish Tinkering (existing gnomes get it on respec).
- **12, 13** The explosion applies position and death, and counts device circles.
- **14** Circle slots are assigned by trying every lower/higher choice, not greedily in typed order.
- **15, 16, 19** The dead per-device cooldown readers are removed; `device info` reads the broken flag; the free slot count is real.
- **17** Broken-device attempts and failed UMD checks take the standard action.
- **18** Build time follows the assigned levels.
- **20** A fourth spell without Brilliance and Blunder is refused.
- **21** The destroy warning says 20 minutes.
- **22** The premade artificer takes Magical Aptitude, Improved Initiative (human), and Empower Spell instead of the free craft feats.
- **23** Artificer Item Creation lets an artificer brew any spell it could put in a device, with no spell slot and ahead of the alchemist level cap; a potion takes the artificer level only for an emulated spell.
- **24** New `class-artificer` help, a rewritten `device` help, and gnome help listing both feats (dev help DB and `help.hlp`).
- **25** The slot table is clamped at level 20.

Items 10, 11, and 23 were design choices; they follow the owner's decisions, recorded in the handoff notes.

## Verification

- New `unittests/CuTest/test_artificer_devices.c` (21 tests); the device, brewing, and metamagic tests fail on the old code.
- `make test`: all suites pass.
- Local CI matrix (`scripts/ci/local/run.py`), including the clang-tidy gate: all jobs pass.
- Live check on port 4100 (private network namespace, disposable database): a new human premade artificer got the alchemist kit and the new feats; at artificer 20, `device add` took haste (3rd circle) and stone skin (4th), a four-spell create was refused, `device create cancel` worked during a build, and the new help entries display.

Handoff notes: `docs/ongoing-projects/ARTIFICER_DEVICE_FIXES.md`.

Closes #4
