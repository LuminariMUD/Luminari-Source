## What changes for players

- **Crew experience.** Every hired hand learns from the work (rooms sailed, reloads and shots at a locked contact, repairs, cargo sold, a hull their ship sinks) and is promoted for free on reaching the next tier. A lost hull's crew gives up about a tenth of its experience and may drop a tier, never below green. Until renown arrives (S7), mortals hire only green hands; `shipcrew` shows each hand's experience and next threshold.
- **Stamina.** Crews tire at the helm, on every shot and reload, and on repairs, and rest faster berthed or at anchor. An exhausted crew turns, accelerates, reloads and repairs more slowly and shoots less straight. `shipstatus` shows it.
- **Repair.** Repair stores (the hull weight) refill at every berth. At sea the crew mends sails, rudder and structure only up to the bosun's caps and never armor; `shiprepair` at sea is one Craft (woodworking) patch. At a shipyard `shiprepair [armor|structure|sails|rudder|weapons|all]` buys priced dock repairs that keep her at the berth. The old free repair is gone, and refits no longer repair (L10).
- **Loss (decision D3).** A player's hull that goes down is rebuilt as the shipyard's cheapest boat, stripped, and waits in the wreck registry with her name, crew, paint and permits. Insurance is automatic (75% of a merchant hull's value, 50% of the rest, 90% when an NPC sank her, nothing for a raft or a rebuilt wreck); `shipinsure` is retired and existing premiums are refunded as claims.
- **`shipsummon`** at a shipyard calls a hull, or her wreck, for a small fee after a passage time (Duris's formula, doubled from the registry, at most 75 minutes). The hull leaves at once and survives a reboot in transit.
- **Trade-in and rename.** `shipbuy <id> trade` rebuilds the owner's berthed hull as the new design for 90% of her value, keeping what of her fit-out the new class can carry. A rename after the first christening costs 10% of her value.

### 1. [P2] Prevent wreck trade-ins from cheaply renewing insurance

Location: [src/vessels/vessels_edit.c:969](https://gitlab.com/max757/Luminari-Source/-/blob/9d0a40de370b98417e9bf5693b4ca60c3b9146cb/src/vessels/vessels_edit.c#L969)

A recovered wreck receives 90% of its replacement boat's full catalog value here, and `vessel_rebuild_hull()` clears `wreck_hull`. An owner can therefore buy the cheapest boat, have another player's ship sink it for the 75% automatic payout, summon the replacement, and use `shipbuy <same-id> trade` to obtain an insured boat again for only 10% of its price. For the default 600-gold boat this pays 450 gold and costs 60 gold to renew the hull, plus the small summon fee, so repeated losses create gold. Exclude recovered wrecks from this credit or account for their prior insurance settlement when pricing a trade-in and renewing coverage.

### 2. [P2] Persist a loss marker in the insurance claim transaction

Location: [src/vessels/vessels_upgrades.c:81](https://gitlab.com/max757/Luminari-Source/-/blob/9d0a40de370b98417e9bf5693b4ca60c3b9146cb/src/vessels/vessels_upgrades.c#L81)

Removing the policy-consumption update leaves this INSERT with no durable guard against issuing another claim for the same loss. Reproduce by saving an insured hull while its sinking timer is running, then terminating the server after this transaction commits but before `vessel_stow()` saves the rebuilt wreck. On restart the saved hull is still sinking and queues a second claim with a new ID; the player's claim-ID high-water mark cannot deduplicate it. Atomically record a consumed loss/generation alongside the claim, or commit the loss-state transition with the claim, so replaying a saved sinking hull cannot pay twice.

### 3. [P2] Retry failed saves before abandoning a stowed hull

Location: [src/vessels/vessels_loss.c:213](https://gitlab.com/max757/Luminari-Source/-/blob/9d0a40de370b98417e9bf5693b4ca60c3b9146cb/src/vessels/vessels_loss.c#L213)

If `vessel_save_one()` fails, this function only logs the failure and then makes the hull inactive. `save_all_vessels()` skips inactive ships, so even after the database recovers, subsequent autosaves and an orderly shutdown never retry a wreck still in the registry. A transient failure of the first save during sinking consequently leaves the pre-loss hull in the database, and reboot restores that hull instead of the wreck; a failed summon save similarly loses its saved destination or due time. Keep stowed hulls eligible for dirty-state retries, and only treat the transition as durably saved after every required write succeeds.

### 4. [P2] Revoke deleted players' permits on stowed hulls too

Location: [src/vessels/vessels_ownership.c:395](https://gitlab.com/max757/Luminari-Source/-/blob/9d0a40de370b98417e9bf5693b4ca60c3b9146cb/src/vessels/vessels_ownership.c#L395)

The newly introduced stowed hulls have `active == FALSE`, so this validity check skips permit removal unless the deleted player also owns that hull. Grant Bob helm access to Alice's ship, stow it, delete Bob, then summon it: the SQL permit is deleted, but the stale in-memory `helm_permits` entry is written back by `vessel_save_one()` during summoning (or a later save). A subsequently created character named Bob then regains helm access because authorization compares names. Include stowed hulls in the permit-pruning loop while retaining the separate cleanup for hulls owned by the deleted player.

### 5. [P2] Make the migration's refund and policy reset atomic

Location: [sql/components/vessels_phase21_schema.sql:36](https://gitlab.com/max757/Luminari-Source/-/blob/9d0a40de370b98417e9bf5693b4ca60c3b9146cb/sql/components/vessels_phase21_schema.sql#L36)

The refund INSERT and the following policy reset run as separate autocommitted statements. With an existing policy, interrupt the migration after the INSERT or make the UPDATE fail: the refund claim remains committed while `insured_for` remains positive. Retrying this migration, or simply booting the server and running `vessel_refund_insurance_premiums()`, creates another independently payable claim for the same premium. Put these two data changes in one transaction after the ALTER statements, matching the runtime refund path.

No new column. Test: `Test_vessel_insurance_claim_marks_the_loss_paid`.
3. **Stowed-hull save retry** (`64f0a70d3`): `vessel_db_save_runtime()` and `vessel_db_save_weapons()` accept a stowed hull, `save_all_vessels()` saves stowed hulls with the fleet (shutdown and staff save), and `vessel_stow()` no longer raises `active` for a single attempt. 5. **Atomic migration refund** (`cbcdc85e2`): the Phase 21 refund INSERT and policy reset run in one `START TRANSACTION` ... `COMMIT` after the ALTERs. With a live policy it refunds once, and a rerun adds no claim.
