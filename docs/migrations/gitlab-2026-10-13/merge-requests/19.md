## Fixes (one commit each)

- `3996cb84b` **Keep a captain's waypoints and routes to that captain.** S12 let only a row's
  creator change it but let every captain use it. That coupled captains two ways: a captain could
  put another's waypoint on a route, and its creator could then never delete it; and a route's
  creator could change the course of another captain's hull scheduled on that route. A captain
  may now sail (`setroute`), schedule (`setschedule`), list, and route through only their own
  waypoints and routes and the harbors' (creator 0). Immortals may use any. Name lookups take
  the caller's own row, then the harbors'. An in-use guard on `addtoroute` was dropped as the
  alternative, because it would let a rival freeze your route by scheduling a hull on it.
- `9ab0bac74` **Give a removed player's waypoints and routes to the staff.** Boot sets
  `top_idnum` from the index, so removing the highest-numbered player hands that ID, and their
  rows, to the next new character.
- `2ad435a40` **Count Connector/C's 5000-range client errors (5013, 5014) as an unanswered
  COMMIT.**
- `d2218eb25` **Stop the manifest writer at a failure only inside a transaction.** `2ba35d6b1`
  made it stop at the first failure everywhere, which lost every later lot for callers that save
  without a transaction.
- `905184ab4` **Create `ship_waypoints` and `ship_routes` with `creator_id`.** Tables made
  through the staff `init vessels` path lacked the column until the next reboot.

Known and tracked, not changed here: the residual after an unanswered COMMIT whose second write
also fails (#12; its log line now names the supply too), freight acceptance's COMMIT (#12, #13),
and staff choosing among same-named player rows (this predates S12).

Help changes (DELWAYPOINT, DELROUTE, ADDTOROUTE, SETROUTE, SETSCHEDULE, LISTROUTES,
LISTWAYPOINTS) are in `help.hlp` and `help_vessel_entries.sql`, and need a production help sync
after the merge. No schema change.
