## Description

Step S3 of the DurisMUD vessel study (`docs/ongoing-projects/vessels-ships.md`): the damage model (3.3.1, 3.3.3, the S3 parts of 3.3.10, and decision D6). The code lives in the new `src/vessels/vessels_damage.c`.

- **Condition profiles:** each class takes its Duris analog's per-arc armor and internal structure at the class beam armor, its sail hit points, and its price (a warship at 109: armor 87/109/65/109, structure 38/47/23/47, 140 sail, 44,000 gold). A prototype's armor (0-229) scales the profile; the shipyard price is the class price times `0.5 + 0.25 * armor / class armor + 0.25 * speed / class speed`.
- **Arcs:** the Duris bands, fore 320-40, starboard 40-140, rear 140-220, port 220-320.
- **Hits:** each fragment (every weapon resolves as a Duris ballista until S4) strikes the sails or the arc facing the shooter across its spread; armor absorbs first, confirmed criticals carry half past it, overkill spills into the structure, a gutted arc deflects one hit in three and damages weapons (disabled at 1, destroyed at 100), stern hits foul the rudder, and one structural hit in nine knocks everyone aboard prone unless they make Reflex DC 15.
- **Holes and sinking:** one holed arc (no armor, no structure) stops a hull dead at once (half speed aloft); two start the sink timer, 75-150 s for an owned hull and 1000-1500 s for an unowned one, which a restart resumes rather than rerolls. A sinking hull cannot move, fire, or be repaired. When she goes down half of each bulk cargo lot floats off as salvage crates, which `shipsalvage` hauls into a stopped hull's hold.
- **Prizes (D6):** `strikecolors`; `claimship` and `plunder` take only a beaten prize (holed, immobile, colors struck, or abandoned at sea with nobody awake aboard), and `claimship` refuses a sinking one; hostile boarding needs the target at speed 3 or less or beaten.
- **Refits:** plating and reinforcement +20%, rigging +10% speed (at least 1, at most 30), hold +25%; each costs a fifth of the class price.
- **Migration (Phase 19):** prototype armor is rescaled once by the class beam armor over the old default (`armor_scale`; a warship of 40 becomes 109). Hulls saved before S3 (`condition_model` 0) are converted once at load: the class profile at the rescaled armor, refits recomputed, and each arc, the sails, and the rudder keeping their damage fraction, with at least 1 structure per arc. `ship_runtime_state.sink_ticks` and `ship_weapons.weapon_damage` are new. The content packages write S3-scale armor and their provisioners apply Phase 19 first.
- **Display:** `shipstatus` adds structure, sails, rudder, holes, the sink timer, struck colors, and each weapon's state.
- Help is updated in `help.hlp` and the SQL components (SHIPFIRE with SHIPSALVAGE and STRIKECOLORS, SHIPSTATUS, SHIPREPAIR, SHIPUPGRADE, CLAIMSHIP, PLUNDER, BOARD_HOSTILE, VEDIT). `VESSEL_SYSTEM.md` has a Damage Model section; `VESSEL_SCHEMA_DEPLOYMENT.md` covers Phase 19.

A breach needs to stop a surface hull immediately. This branch makes `vessel_max_speed()` zero, but `vessel_movement_tick_one()` still calls `vessel_sail_tick()`, which decelerates the current `ship->speed` by only the class acceleration and then advances `speed / 90` rooms. For example, a warship holed at speed 17 moves at speed 15.5 on the next tick and can cross several room edges before stopping; a hull holed on two sides also keeps moving while its sink timer runs. This violates S3 3.3.3 (one breach immobilizes; a sinking hull cannot move). Clear current speed or prevent travel as soon as the breach/sinking state starts.

### Discussion ece72c7179a42975f7a5b46ce68bba1edb28bf9d

#### Note 3924309923

Max aka Mosheh (max757) at 2026-09-29T14:39:32.097Z. System event: no. Resolved: True.

Review location: src/vessels/vessels.h; old line None; new line 1546; head d98ee1b198a087c77ecc50a95ee0387436f016ac.

The remaining sink time is runtime-only even though damaged hull condition is persisted. On boot, `load_all_ship_interiors()` calls `vessel_update_condition()` for a two-breach hull, which rolls a new 75-150 second (owned) or 1000-1500 second (unowned) timer. A ship saved with one second left therefore gets a full new countdown after a normal restart; repeated restarts can keep it afloat indefinitely. S3 3.3.3 promises a bounded time from the second breach to sinking. Persist the deadline or remaining ticks and restore it without rerolling.
