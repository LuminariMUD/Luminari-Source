## What changes for players

- **Raiders.** A moving player's hull bigger than a boat, on open water with no lock set, is ambushed about once in 17 minutes of sailing (1 in 2002 ticks): twice as often in a pirate cove, half as often in territorial waters, and sixty times more rarely under neutral colors. Ships and transports are ambushed at most once a voyage. The hull's weight picks one of four raider tiers and a pirate or a hunter. The raider appears beyond sight off the bow and closes at full speed.
- **Raider AI** (Duris `NPCShipAI`). The basic brain turns the arc ready soonest onto the quarry and keeps within its weapons' band; the advanced brain projects both hulls 3 s ahead and works round to the quarry's weakest side with a broadside. Raiders fire as NPC return fire, ram when it pays, run when out of ammunition or holed, and board a slow quarry through the existing boarding contest. Pirates loot part of the hold and leave; hunters fight on. Killing the captain on her bridge stops her; the strongbox key is on the captain. A raider that loses her quarry leaves the sea 600 ticks later once nobody watches. Raiders cannot be claimed and never survive a restart.
- **NPC merchants** under fire now run from their attacker while returning fire, then resume their route.
- **Ramming.** `shipram [off]` braces the crew to ram the locked contact at speed 6 or more, with Duris's bow cone, chance, crash damage, fitted ram, knockdowns, and cooldowns. The sailmaster trains on every ram attempt.
- **Staff.** `vesseldebug raider <0-3> [hunter]` launches a raider against the player's hull you are aboard.

[P1] Revalidate consent for the hull actually rammed

At speed 6 or more, lock an unowned NPC hull and issue `shipram` while it is still more than one room away, then use `shiplock` to select a nearby player-owned hull whose owner is offline or has PvP disabled. `vessel_lock_contact()` changes `lock_target` without checking PvP consent or clearing `ramming`, and this tick passes the new target directly to `vessel_ram()`. The consent check in `do_shipram()` therefore protects only the original target: a normal player can damage or sink a protected player's ship. Bind the armed ram to the authorized actor and target, and cancel or revalidate consent when the lock changes and before resolving the impact.

[P2] Retire existing raiders before deleting their tier mappings

Run this rollback while a Corsair hull still has a `ship_runtime_state` row. This deletion removes its entry from `vessel_raider_tiers`, while the later `NOT EXISTS` guard deliberately retains its prototype because the hull is persisted. On restart, `vessel_raider_boot()` identifies raiders solely through `SELECT DISTINCT prototype_id FROM vessel_raider_tiers`, so it cannot find or retire that hull. Since `raider_mode` is runtime-only, the restored hull also loses its raider despawn and capture restrictions and remains an ordinary public hull, contrary to the rollback's promised restart cleanup. Retire the affected live and persisted hulls before removing the mappings, or retain a durable retirement marker until boot cleanup has completed.

Reviewed commit: `648dd94516c9791dcf9cd26ea152768de7ec2075`. Validation: pinned static source review; build, database migrations, and live game tests were not run.
