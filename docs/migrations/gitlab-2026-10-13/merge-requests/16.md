## Description

S10 of the vessel study (`docs/ongoing-projects/vessels-ships.md`, Phase 10, the last step of
Part 5): the illustrated player guide, written from S9's play notes and screenshots, and the
defects found while checking every fact in it against the code.

Review range: `git log vessels-s10-base..vessels-s10`.

## The guide

`docs/guides/VESSEL_PLAYER_GUIDE.md` follows a new captain through the whole system in the order
a player meets it: passage on public ships, finding and buying a hull, crew and refits, sailing,
routes and autopilot, trade and freight, gunnery, damage and repair, boarding and prizes, two
captains (permits, PvP, renown, bounties), contraband and customs, loss and recovery, other hulls
and vehicles, the living world (events, the derelict, hunters) and client data, with a command
summary and a staff appendix.

The screenshots moved from `docs/ongoing-projects/guide-screenshots/` to
`docs/guides/vessel-guide/`: ten are cropped to the lines that matter, five have staff staging
lines or old interior minimaps blanked, and three (two duplicates and one that shows a refused
shot rather than its note) are removed. The master index and `VESSEL_SYSTEM.md` link to the guide.

Two passes checked the facts: four parallel audits of the screenshots and the notes against the
code before writing, and a fresh review of the finished guide after. Every number and rule the
guide states is the code's.
