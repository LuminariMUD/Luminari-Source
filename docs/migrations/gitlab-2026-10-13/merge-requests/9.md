## Description

Step S4 of the DurisMUD vessel study (`docs/ongoing-projects/vessels-ships.md`): weapons and gunnery (3.3.4, the S4 parts of 3.3.1 and 3.3.2, the default armament of 3.3.10, L6, and the S4 half of L9). The catalogue and the shipyard live in the new `src/vessels/vessels_weapons.c`, the gunnery in the new `src/vessels/vessels_gunnery.c`.

- **Catalogue:** the twelve Duris weapons (small/medium/large ballista, small/medium/large catapult, heavy ballista, light/heavy beamcannon, Mind Blast Cannon, fragmentation cannon, Long Tom) at 2 gold per platinum, plus a ram and neutral colors. Reloads were tuned by the duel harness from Duris's 30 s and 45 s to 20 s and 30 s.
- **Slots:** 16 per hull (type, arc, catalogue row, ammo, damage, reload timer: 8 bytes each, inside the 5 KiB struct budget). A new hull gets its class armament: large ballistae on a warship's bow and beams, a medium ballista on the bow of the other armed classes.
- **Class fitting (3.3.1):** hull weight, the Duris analog's mounts and weight cap per arc, and its allowed weapons. One legality check (arcs, mounts, caps, one capital weapon, the class weight budget) refuses both a purchase and a departure.
- **Shipyard:** `shipweapon list|buy|sell|swap`, `shipequip list|buy|sell`, `shiprearm [slot|all]`. A capital weapon needs a veteran gunner until renown arrives in S7. Installation and rearming keep the shipwrights at work (75 s per weight point, 75 s per weapon), which blocks departure; immortals skip it. Unowned hulls rearm when they berth.
- **Gunnery (L6):** `shiplock` a contact, then `shipfire <slot|arc>`. A shot hits on `d20 + bonus >= DC`, with `DC = 21 - round(20 * h)` and `h` Duris's `weaponsight()` chance after its 2d50 roll (crossing speed, bearing swing, closing speed with both hulls projected one second ahead through `vessel_sail_tick()`, target size, range, x1.5 miss against a hull aloft). The bonus is the gunner tier plus DEX (INT for ballistic weapons), at most +7; NPC crews fire at their tier +5. Criticals threaten by pierce; reload shortens with the gunner tier; weapons fire only on the arc facing the target and inside their range band. NPC return fire uses the same rules.
- **Battle stations (L9, 3.3.2):** a lock or a shot, fired or received, puts a crew at battle stations until 180 s after the lock clears. At battle stations a hull is refused harbors and shallows, and any other refused room rolls Duris's crash check; a grounding lands `hull weight / 25 + 1` hits.
- **Mind Blast Cannon:** a hit stuns the target crew for 5-20 s (no steering, firing, reloading, or repair); inside mid-range everyone aboard saves (Will 15) or falls prone.
- **Flight:** every vessel range counts one room per 10 Z; an airborne hull is boarded only within 10 Z; submerged hulls neither fire nor are targeted.
- **`shipsight`, `shipscan`, `contacts`:** the DC and chance per weapon against the lock; a contact's armor, structure, and weapons within 20 rooms; the arc each contact lies off and the lock.
- **Persistence (Phase 20):** every slot is a `ship_weapons` row with `catalog_id` and `ammo`; the `slot_data` blob and its hex codec are gone. Weapon rows saved before S4 load once as the class default weapon on the same arc with full ammunition, keeping their damage.
- **Duel harness (D2):** `vesseldebug balance` sails two default warships with Duris's frigate combat fit through the production movement, gunnery, damage, and reload code: 200 duels give a median of 431 s, p95 590 s, minimum 262 s, one draw (1,000: 434/604/249 s, five draws).
- **Found on the way (older than S4):** a replacement merchant spawned facing north; at the Vailand north port the S2 route check sailed it east onto the beach, so no replacement Ironwind Trader could enter service. Merchants now spawn on the bearing of their first leg (`cee7dcdcb`).
- Help is updated in `help.hlp` and the SQL components (SHIPFIRE with SHIPLOCK, SHIPSIGHT, SHIPSCAN; SHIPHIRE with SHIPWEAPON, SHIPEQUIP, SHIPREARM; SHIPSTATUS, CONTACTS, BOARD_HOSTILE, SPEED, UNDOCK, VESSELDEBUG). `VESSEL_SYSTEM.md` has a Weapons and Gunnery (S4) section; `VESSEL_SCHEMA_DEPLOYMENT.md` covers Phase 20.

The pinned change introduces three actionable defects that allow shipyard maintenance, crew-stun restrictions, and target protection to be bypassed.

**Overall impact: high.** This replaces weapon storage and combat behavior for existing and newly created vessels, migrates persisted loadouts, and changes movement, harbor access, and NPC shipping behavior, affecting ship owners, passengers, naval combatants, and schema deployment.

1. **[P2] Keep the vessel berthed when maintenance starts** -- [src/vessels/vessels_weapons.c:383](https://gitlab.com/max757/Luminari-Source/-/blob/10da1aa76ddb97cc51503813a2a06de42b92fdcc/src/vessels/vessels_weapons.c#L383)

   An owner can issue `undock` and then buy a weapon or rearm during the 30-second departure countdown. `vessel_refit_ship()` checks only whether the hull is physically in port, so it accepts the purchase, but adding maintenance neither cancels the pending departure nor prevents `vessel_departure_tick()` from clearing `dock`. The vessel can therefore sail with hundreds of maintenance seconds remaining; buying after casting off finishes but before leaving the port also bypasses the restriction. Starting maintenance needs to preserve the berth and block an already-started departure, not just future calls to `vessel_begin_departure()`.

2. **[P2] Apply crew stun to manual vessel maneuvers** -- [src/vessels/vessels_movement.c:975](https://gitlab.com/max757/Luminari-Source/-/blob/10da1aa76ddb97cc51503813a2a06de42b92fdcc/src/vessels/vessels_movement.c#L975)

   The stun guard only covers movement through `vessel_sail_tick()`. A pilot who passes the mental blast's Will save can still use `setsail <direction>` while the crew is stunned: `do_greyhawk_setsail()` calls `vessel_maneuver()`, which never checks the stun and immediately changes position, heading, and speed when the hull is moving at speed 6 or less. `setsail up/down` also remains available. This lets players steer or stop the hull during the cannon's intended loss of control; the maneuver and altitude paths need the same crew-stun restriction.

3. **[P2] Revalidate a cached lock before firing** -- [src/vessels/vessels_gunnery.c:735](https://gitlab.com/max757/Luminari-Source/-/blob/10da1aa76ddb97cc51503813a2a06de42b92fdcc/src/vessels/vessels_gunnery.c#L735)

   When no contact argument is supplied, `vessel_locked_target()` checks only that the fleet slot is active. If a locked submarine dives, or an unfired-upon target enters port, `shipfire <slot>` before the next gunnery tick can still damage it: the remaining weapon and consent checks do not call `vessel_target_problem()`. The tick eventually clears the lock, but it leaves a window in which submerged or harbor-protected vessels can be fired upon, whereas the previous command checked harbor protection on every shot. Recheck target protection and visibility when firing through an existing lock.
