## Description

Step S2 of the DurisMUD vessel study (`docs/ongoing-projects/vessels-ships.md`): movement and pacing (D1).

- **Momentum:** orders set a speed and heading; every 0.5 s vessel tick the hull converges on them at its class acceleration and turn rate, scaled by the sailmaster and the rudder, and covers speed / 90 rooms (speed 10 crosses a room every 4.5 s). Its position inside the room is kept; it crosses room edges in the order its track meets them (diagonally only through a corner), and each room entered is validated; a refused room stops the hull where its track met the edge.
- **Maximum speed:** design speed times the sailmaster, load, sail-condition, and terrain/weather/lane factors, SEADOG +1 at the helm, capped at 30; the class table sets the design speeds for new prototypes.
- **Moorings:** a hull at rest in port is berthed; `undock` casts off in 30 s, the new `anchor` holds a hull at sea and `undock` weighs it in 13 s. `setsail` is now the one-room maneuver (speed 6 or less, 5 s cooldown; `up`/`down` change altitude or depth).
- **Automated movers:** the autopilot steers (comes about in place when the waypoint is astern, slows for stop waypoints, casts off by itself); hunters steer and shadow their target; merchants cruise at design speed; `setschedule` and every scheduled departure validate the route by sailing a copy of the hull through the same steering and physics. New waypoints are reached on entering their own room. A hull that comes to rest saves its runtime row. Unowned hulls get their rigging made good when they berth.
- **Removed:** the old per-tick room step, manual grounding, and the draft barrier (the 3.3.2 crash check moves to S4, the refit rescaling to S3).
- **Also fixed:** `reglist type 5` and `pathlist`, broken since `500019db1`.
- Help is updated in `help.hlp` and the SQL components (VESSELS, AUTOPILOT, SEADOG, new ANCHOR keyword); `VESSEL_SYSTEM.md` has a Movement and Pacing section.

The physical path crosses the east edge first, then the north edge, but `vessel_cross_room_edges()` calls `vessel_enter_cell()` only for the northeast cell. The new corner test covers only an exact 45-degree crossing. **Fix:** retain the pre-tick offsets and process boundary crossings in time order, checking each entered cell; use a diagonal step only when the two boundary times coincide.
