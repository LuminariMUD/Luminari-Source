Familiar HP investigation - issue #270
2026-10-28

The supplied transcript uses summon and shows a level 9 hellhound at -8/101
HP. summon calls char_pets_to_char_loc(): it transports existing charmed
followers and does not create or heal them. An existing familiar can therefore
arrive mortally wounded. call familiar also recalls the existing follower
before considering creation or cooldowns. Saved pets retain their current HP;
prepare_saved_pet_row() accepts -8 HP because it is mortally wounded, not dead.
The report does not establish when or how this particular pet lost its HP.

Local investigation used the authored lib/world/mob/0.mob prototypes and the
real do_call()/perform_call()/read_mobile() path. All ten familiar selections,
caster levels 1 through 30, and Improved Familiar ranks 0 through 5 were checked:
1,800 fresh calls started at full, positive HP. This temporary investigation
test was removed because the full world file is external game data, not a
tracked dependency of the portable test suite.

Damage remains persistent, including negative HP on a living pet. Recalling,
logging in, or reclaiming a saved pet must not become a free heal. An injured
arrival now tells its owner the current and maximum HP and that healing is
needed. Nonpositive HP is reported as critically wounded. This applies to all
pet types, including familiars. Healthy arrivals and automatic travel produce
no injury message. New familiar creation still fills HP as before.

Production-linked regression coverage:
- Test_gameplay_familiar_recall_reports_injuries_without_changing_health
  exercises summon, familiar recall, and automatic travel at 101, 17, 0, and
  -8 HP out of 101. It checks HP, position, ownership, and injury feedback.
- Test_gameplay_companion_creation_sets_bond_and_preserves_failed_call_cooldown
  also checks that a freshly created familiar is at full, positive HP.
- Test_pet_familiar_restore_reports_critical_injury_without_healing uses a
  connection-local temporary pet schema to save and restore a familiar at
  -8/101 HP and check the mortally wounded position and owner warning.

The recall regression failed on missing feedback before the fix. These checks
do not establish the original damage source or reproduce the player's complete
prior combat/login sequence. No production server or player records were changed.
