Pet Phase 1 final acceptance - 2026-09-09
=========================================

Scope
-----

This is the delivery evidence record for the remaining Phase 1 workflows.  It
does not add a new implementation phase or checklist.  Scopeguard and
plan-ablation kept the work to observed failures and the shortest existing
player paths.  Offline expiry, restart durability, and persistence redesign
remain Phase 2 work.

Production-linked acceptance
----------------------------

The final root suite exercised the production sources, including:

- all authored construct recipes, corpse conversion, failed-roll consumption,
  missing-prototype and admission retention, repair, one-time dismantling, and
  the documented craft create golem animate corpse command;
- bonded recall without stat or resource refill, Boon Companion, familiar
  benefits, ranger bonuses, eidolon evolutions, undead cohort progression,
  mercenary hit points, and respec/keeper round trips;
- lesser and elite animated forms, spellcasting forms, weighted capacity,
  incorporeal equipment rejection, Deathless Touch, eligible direct-kill
  autoraise, blocked/player/used/summoned corpses, and anti-farming ownership;
- artifact, moonblade, totem, spell, feat, class, corpse, purchase, and quest
  award admission before non-intentional resource commitment;
- pet ownership, loyal orders, non-orderable illusions, damage attribution,
  owner feedback, PvP protection, dismissal, expiry, death, transfer,
  separation/recovery, nested gear, and failed-save safety; and
- mount anatomy, size, ability and control restrictions, riding, travel,
  combat, dismount, recall, extraction, and the rule that riding grants no
  ownership or command rights.

The quest award is production-linked through complete_quest and covers valid,
missing, full-roster, invalid, retry, duplicate, and race-change cases.  The
installed world has no authored follower-reward quest to run live.  Adding an
unrelated quest solely as a test fixture would create new game content outside
Phase 1; the actual quest resolution handler is therefore the bounded proof.
