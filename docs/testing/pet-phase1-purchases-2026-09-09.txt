Live acceptance on port 4100
----------------------------

- Gold: zero-balance denial did not spend currency; a 1000-gold producing-stock
  token created an owned General-role rogue; full-capacity denial retained the
  remaining balance; order and dismissal succeeded; producing stock remained
  available.
- Quest points: the same denial, successful purchase, ownership, capacity,
  order, dismissal, and producing-stock checks succeeded with a temporary
  quest-point flag.
- Account experience: the same checks succeeded with a temporary
  account-experience flag.  The initial live pass exposed that shop.c used the
  in-memory-only change_account_experience helper.  The final build uses
  account.c:change_account_xp, and two 1000-XP purchases persisted the database
  balance from 2000 to the recorded baseline of 0.
- Disconnect and reconnect after the paid workflow remained healthy.  The
  original crash was delayed until unrelated movement caused the periodic
  owner registry to traverse a freed temporary character.
